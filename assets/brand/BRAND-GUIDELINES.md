# Metrale — brand guidelines

## The mark

The M is the brand. Two chevron-topped stems joined by a 45° V; the left half in
ink, the right half in violet; a cyan bar beneath the V; and the gold swash —
the comma that closes the wordmark — lifted above the right shoulder when the M
stands alone.

- **`mark`** — avatars, app icons, favicons above 48 px. Minimum 24 px wide.
- **`mark-compact`** — the M without the swash. Favicons at 48 px and below;
  minimum 16 px. Never use it above 48 px — the swash is part of the mark.
- **`wordmark`** — the default logo. Minimum 140 px wide.
- **`lockup-<product>`** — wordmark with the product name beneath. Minimum
  240 px wide. **`-inline`** puts the product after the swash on one line for
  header strips and banners.

## Metrale AI

The AI glyph is drawn, not typed: a Λ with the M's construction (ink left,
violet right, cyan crossbar floating inside) and a plain stem for the I. Use
`lockup-ai` / `lockup-ai-inline` wherever another product would use its typed
lockup, and `ai-mark` where the product needs an icon of its own. The gold
swash sits on the I only when the glyph stands alone. Don't set "AI" in type
next to the wordmark except via `lockup-ai-inline-type`, and don't mix the two
treatments on one surface.

## Company and products

Metrale is the company; products take their place under the name. One lockup
per surface — never the company wordmark and a product lockup together. Every
product uses the same Urbanist Regular at the same size and gray; the name is
the only thing that changes.

## Clear space

Keep at least one unit — half the cyan bar's width, 76 units, about a fifth of
the M's height — clear on all sides. Nothing else sits inside that box.
`svg/spec/clear-space.svg` shows it.

## Colour

The brand ground is `#0E1318`; the kit is designed for it and the dark cuts are
the primary ones. On white, the ink turns `#15181F` and the product name
`#6B7079`; violet, cyan and gold stay. The `-mono` cuts are for single-colour
reproduction (print, engraving, embossing): everything in ink, no gradients.

Don't recolour the pieces, swap the gradient directions, or set the M in one
colour while keeping colour elsewhere.

## Type

Urbanist (Google Fonts). Medium for headings and the wordmark, Regular for
product names and body copy. Fall back to Helvetica Neue / Helvetica.

## Don't

- Don't rebuild the letters in another typeface — the outlines are the artwork.
- Don't remove the swash from the wordmark. It is the comma; it is what says
  "and then" before a product name.
- Don't put the swash anywhere but its two positions: after the letters, or
  above the M's right shoulder.
- Don't stretch, skew, outline, shadow or glow any piece.
- Don't place the logo on a photograph or a busy ground without a clear panel
  of ground colour behind it.
- Don't use the light-ground cuts on mid-tone grounds; if the ground is darker
  than 50 % gray, use the on-dark cuts.

## The engine

This repository is the Metrale Engine. The kit has no engine lockup
(`src/products.json` lists Full Stack and AI), so the engine's surfaces use the
plain `wordmark` and name the product in type — "Metrale Engine", "The Metrale
Engine Book" — never a hand-made `lockup-engine`. Legal lines read
"© 2026 Metrale Corp.".

## Files

Everything is generated by `gen.js` from `src/geometry.js` (the drawing),
`src/paths.json` (the letter outlines) and `src/products.json` (the product
list). This directory carries the kit's `svg/`, `src/`, `tokens/`, `social/`
and `gen.js`; its raster renders (`dark/`, `light/`, `transparent/`,
`templates/`) stay with the kit, and only the cuts the web properties serve
are copied into `site/static/` and `blog/static/`.

What reads it here:

| file | what it feeds |
| --- | --- |
| `src/geometry.js`, `src/paths.json` | `node site/scripts/brand/lockup.mjs` writes `web-shared/brand-art.js` (what `MetraleLockup.svelte` draws), the plain wordmark masters `svg/wordmark*.svg`, and the book's menu-bar wordmark `book/theme/metrale.js` |
| `tokens/brand.json` | `web-shared/metrale-tokens.css` restates it for the site, the blog and the book; `site/src/lib/brand-tokens.test.js` holds the two equal |
| `svg/mark-compact.svg` | served as `/brand/mark-compact.svg` (a link from `site/static/brand/`) |
| `svg/wordmark-ondark.svg` | the book's menu bar, and the social cards |
| `social/og-image-dark.png` | `site/static/og-image.png` and `blog/static/og-image.png` |
| `social/github-social-preview-*.png` | uploaded by hand in the repository settings |

When the kit changes: replace `src/` and `svg/` (and `tokens/`, `social/`),
run `node site/scripts/brand/lockup.mjs`, copy the new icon cuts into both
`static/` directories, and run `bun test --preload ./test-runes.js src/lib` in
`site/` — `lockup-artwork`, `brand-tokens`, `theme-color` and the contrast
tests name everything that has to follow. `site/BRANDING.md` says how the
website applies the kit.
