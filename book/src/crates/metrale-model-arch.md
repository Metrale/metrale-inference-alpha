# metrale-model-arch

**Path:** `crates/model-arch/`

Per-family architectures (GLM-5 Next, DeepSeek V4.1, Nemotron, Kimi K3, DFlash/MTP heads) and their weight loaders. `ModelWeightLoader` and the per-family loaders are in `src/weight_loader/`; see [metrale-model-engine](./metrale-model-engine.md) for how a loader is selected.
